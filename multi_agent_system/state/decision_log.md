# Decision Log

**Purpose**: Record architectural decisions and their rationale  
**Format**: ADR (Architectural Decision Record) style

---

## Decision Template

```markdown
## ADR-XXX: [Title]

**Date**: YYYY-MM-DD  
**Status**: Proposed | Accepted | Deprecated | Superseded  
**Author**: agent_YYYYMMDD_HHMMSS_<role>
**Related Tasks**: TASK-001, TASK-002

### Context
What is the issue that we're seeing that is motivating this decision?

### Decision
What is the change that we're proposing and/or doing?

### Consequences
What becomes easier or more difficult to do because of this change?
- Positive consequences
- Negative consequences
- Neutral consequences

### Alternatives Considered
- Alternative 1: [description] - Why not chosen
- Alternative 2: [description] - Why not chosen
```
